Advanced search syntax

Strict (default): your words are required together in each paper's title/abstract — tight, on-topic results. Broad (Strict off): PubMed adds synonyms and MeSH — wider, but can drift.

Operators always work and override Strict: AND OR NOT (uppercase), "exact phrase", term[tiab] title/abstract, term[mesh], author[au], 2020[dp].

Example: glioblastoma AND temozolomide[tiab] NOT pediatric
